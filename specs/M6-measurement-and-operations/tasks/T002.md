## Ask

SRE grants or removes operations roles

Sam changes an operations person's role with a reason, every grant, removal and refused attempt is logged, and nobody else (not even Sam on his own account) can change a role.

- Slice: vertical slice (the riskiest: auth, a row lock, idempotency and append-only grants) — S12 Operations access at EP-12 `/ops/access` (FE-19 OperationsAccessPage with its role dialog, ST-11) → EP-36 GET /api/ops/staff (`listStaff`) and EP-37 PUT /api/ops/staff/{userId}/role (`changeRole`) (BE-1, BE-5 Idempotency-Key, BE-4 `assertCanChangeRole`, BE-18 StaffAccessService `listStaff` and `changeRole` in lld.md's order of checks) → `users` (role, role_changed_at) and the new `access_log` table → the saved role and the S12 confirmation (SEQ-10)
- FR-054: "When the SRE grants or removes an operations role, the system shall add an access log entry showing who acted, for whom, which role, when and the reason."
- FR-055: "If anyone other than the SRE tries to grant or remove an operations role, then the system shall refuse and leave every role unchanged."
- FR-056: "If the SRE tries to grant or remove a role on their own account, then the system shall refuse and leave their roles unchanged."
- AE-051: "Given Sam Reyes is the SRE, When he grants Dana the data analyst role with the reason "new hire", Then the access log shows Sam, Dana, data analyst, the time and "new hire"."
- AE-052: "Given Fran is the finance lead, When she tries to grant Omar the SRE role, Then she is refused and Omar's roles are unchanged."
- AE-053: "Given Sam is the SRE, When he tries to give himself the finance lead role, Then he is refused and his roles are unchanged."
