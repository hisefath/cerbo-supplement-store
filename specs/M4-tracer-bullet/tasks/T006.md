## Ask

Sign in and idle sign-out

Everyone signs in under their own account; nobody signed in sees an order, a session idle for 15 minutes ends, and the notice leads through sign-in to the order.

- Slice: vertical slice — EP-1 page /sign-in (FE-2 SignInPage) and BE-2 PageGuards (303 to /sign-in?next=) → EP-15, EP-16, EP-17 with the 15-minute idle limit (BE-3, BE-20 Clock) → S8 and the idle countdown (FE-1, ST-2)
- FR-039: "When a person signs in with their own account, the system shall show only the pages their role allows: a patient their own orders, a provider their practice, platform staff the platform pages."
- FR-040: "If someone who is not signed in opens an order, price, stock or dashboard page, then the system shall show the sign-in page and none of that page's contents."
- FR-041: "When a signed-in person has been inactive for 15 minutes, the system shall sign them out and show the sign-in page."
- FR-042: "When a patient signs in after opening an order notice, the system shall open the order the notice was about."
- AE-038: "Given nobody is signed in, When someone opens the link to Ana's order, Then they see the sign-in page and nothing about the order."
- AE-039: "Given Dr. Chen is signed in, When 15 minutes pass with no activity, Then she is signed out and sees the sign-in page."
- AE-040: "Given Dr. Chen sends Ana's $60.00 order, When Ana opens the notice and signs in, Then the notice reads "Willow Creek Wellness sent you a supplement order. Sign in to view and pay." and her $60.00 order opens."
- Also (verifier review of this task, added at G4 re-approval "a, widen T006 Owns and relaunch"):
  - Mount the idle countdown (ST-2) in AppShell (FE-1) so an idle person is moved to the sign-in page without clicking (FR-041), and reset its timer whenever the person's own API calls move the server's `last_seen_at` (otherwise an active person is shown "signed out" while the server session is live).
  - Build the EP-15 rate limit exactly as lld.md §4 states: more than 10 sign-in attempts in 15 minutes for one email or one address answers 429 `/problems/rate-limited`; keep the counts in a database table created by this task's migration (the acceptance helpers empty every table before each test).
  - Accept only `application/json` request bodies in server/utils/problem.ts body parsing (openapi requestBody is JSON only), closing login CSRF through text/plain form posts.
  - The `next=` sign-in parameter: accept only same-site paths (reject anything Nuxt's navigateTo would refuse, such as `/\t/evil.example`), and never report a failed redirect after a successful sign-in as "You're offline".
  - The page guard fails closed: if the session check errors, send the person to sign-in rather than render the page.
  - After sign-in with no `next`, send each role to its home per docs/design/flows.md F7 step 2 (a patient to her orders, not `/`).
  - Unit tests for the `next=` check and for EP-16 sign-out (204, cookie cleared, session ended).
- Also, second review (approval "a, one more T006 run"):
  - The rate limit must hold under parallel attempts: a burst of simultaneous sign-ins for one email or one address may not get more than 10 tries in 15 minutes (record the attempt before counting, or count and record under one lock, in one transaction).
  - The address used for the limit: when the setting `TRUST_PROXY=1` is present, take the client address from the platform proxy's forwarded header; otherwise use the connection address. A request whose address is unknown must not share one count with every other such request.
  - Before the idle countdown sends a person to the signed-out page, it asks EP-17 whether the session really ended; activity in another tab or a skewed client clock must not sign out a live session.
  - A unit test that fails if the limit's "same email OR same address" condition became AND.
