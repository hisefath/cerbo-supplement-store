Patient pays once, split recorded

Ana starts checkout and pays with the test payment; the order is paid and its split is recorded to the cent in the order and the ledger, and the database refuses any later change.

- FR-007: "The system shall label the payment step as a test payment that moves no real money."
- FR-010: "When an order is marked paid, the system shall record its split as item cost, provider payout and platform fee, each in whole cents."
- FR-011: "The system shall compute the platform fee once per order as 0.75% of the amount the patient paid, rounded half-up to the nearest cent."
- FR-012: "The system shall compute the provider payout as the amount paid minus the item cost minus the platform fee."

- FR-031: "The system shall record on every order its practice, provider, time sent and time paid."

- AE-011: "Given an order whose amount paid is $14.00 for items costing $10.00, When its split is computed, Then the fee is $0.11 (0.75% of $14.00 is $0.105, rounded half-up) and the payout is $3.89."
