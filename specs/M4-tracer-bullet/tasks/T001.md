- FR-001: "When a provider starts an order for a patient of their practice, the system shall list the practice's products with each product's current patient price and units in stock."
- FR-002: "When a provider adds a product and a quantity to an order, the system shall show the line price, the order total and the provider's payout after the platform fee."
- FR-017: "The system shall start every product at a default patient price of its cost plus a 50% margin until the practice changes it."
- FR-043: "When a provider sends an order, the system shall send the patient a stand-in notice that names the practice and asks them to sign in to view and pay."
- FR-044: "The system shall leave item names, quantities, amounts and health details out of every notice to a patient."
- AE-001: "Given Dr. Chen starts an order for Ana Ruiz, When she adds 2 Vitamin D3 and 1 Magnesium Glycinate, Then she sees lines of $36.00 and $24.00, a total of $60.00 and her payout of $29.55 after the $0.45 platform fee."
- AE-015: "Given Willow Creek has never changed the price of Omega-3, which costs $15.00, When Dr. Chen starts an order, Then Omega-3 shows at its default price of $30.00 (cost plus a 50% margin)."
- AE-041: "Given the notice for Ana's $60.00 order of Vitamin D3 and Magnesium Glycinate, When anyone reads it, Then it contains no item name, quantity or amount."
