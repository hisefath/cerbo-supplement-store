## Ask

Provider changes a practice price

Dr. Chen changes a product's price by a new price or a margin, sees the lowest allowed price when she goes too low, and every change is kept in a history nobody can edit.

- Slice: tracer bullet — S5 Practice prices (FE-13, FE-14, ST-6) → EP-27 GET /api/practice/prices and EP-28 PUT /api/practice/prices/{productId} (BE-1, BE-4 role provider, BE-5 Idempotency-Key, BE-8 PricingService listPrices and changePrice, BE-6 priceFromMargin and lowestAllowedPriceCents) → `practice_prices` and the new `price_changes` table (append-only, grants per INV-4) → the new price and a history line on S5 (SEQ-4)
- FR-018: "When a provider submits the price-change form with either a new price or a margin percentage, the system shall set the practice's price for that product to the resulting price in whole cents, reading margin as a share of the patient price."
- FR-019: "When a practice's price for a product changes, the system shall add a price history entry showing who changed it, when, the old price, the new price and the reason given."
- FR-020: "If a submitted price would leave the provider a payout below $0.00 on one unit after the platform fee, then the system shall refuse the change and show the lowest allowed price."
- FR-021: "If anyone tries to edit or delete a price history entry, then the system shall refuse and keep the entry unchanged."
- AE-016: "Given Omega-3 costs $15.00 and is priced at $30.00, When Dr. Chen submits the price-change form with a margin of 60% and the reason "new protocol pricing", Then Omega-3's price becomes $37.50 and the price history shows Dr. Chen, the time, $30.00, $37.50 and "new protocol pricing"."
- AE-017: "Given Vitamin D3 is priced at $18.00, When Dr. Chen submits the price-change form with a new price of $19.50, Then Vitamin D3's price becomes $19.50."
- AE-018: "Given Magnesium Glycinate costs $12.00, When Dr. Chen submits a new price of $12.00, Then the change is refused and the form shows $12.09 as the lowest allowed price."
- AE-019: "Given the price history entry from AE-016, When anyone tries to delete it, Then it is refused and the entry still shows $30.00 → $37.50."
- Also: the stale-form check (`expected_current_price_cents`, 409 `/problems/stale-price` with `currentPriceCents` and `changedBy`), validation (exactly one of price or margin, a reason of 1-200 characters) and the per-practice scope (a provider changes only their own practice's price).
- Out of this task: showing a change to patients on their orders (M5-T003); stock (M5-T002, M5-T004); the dashboard (M5-T005); the S5 link in the app menu (M5-T006).
