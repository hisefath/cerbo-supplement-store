## Ask

Patients see price change notices

Ana sees which items changed price on an order she has not started paying for, keeps the prices she saw once checkout starts, and gets the current prices back with a notice after 30 minutes.

- Slice: vertical slice — EP-21 GET /api/orders/{orderId} (BE-9 OrderService: `price_changes` notices from the `price_changes` table for a sent order, held prices while the hold is live, BE-9.expireHoldIfDue after `prices_held_until`) → S2 notice and totals (FE-7) (SM-1, SEQ-2)
- FR-022: "When an item's price changes while an order containing it is not yet in checkout, the system shall show the person viewing that order a notice naming the item, its old price and its new price."
- FR-023: "When a patient starts checkout, the system shall fix every item's price in that order at the price shown at that moment."
- FR-024: "While an order is in checkout, when its practice's price for an item changes, the system shall charge the patient the price fixed at checkout start."
- FR-037: "When 30 minutes pass after a patient started checkout without paying, the system shall return the order to the practice's current prices."
- AE-020: "Given Ana has a sent, unpaid order with Magnesium Glycinate at $24.00 and has not started checkout, When Dr. Chen changes its price to $26.00, Then Ana's order shows a notice "Magnesium Glycinate: $24.00 → $26.00" and a total of $62.00."
- AE-021: "Given Ana started checkout with a total of $60.00, When Dr. Chen changes Vitamin D3 to $20.00 before Ana pays, Then Ana is charged $60.00."
- AE-035: "Given Ana started checkout at 14:00 with Magnesium Glycinate fixed at $24.00 and Dr. Chen then changed it to $26.00, When Ana returns at 14:31 without having paid, Then her order shows $26.00 with a notice "Magnesium Glycinate: $24.00 → $26.00"."
- Out of this task: changing prices (M5-T001); stock (M5-T002).
