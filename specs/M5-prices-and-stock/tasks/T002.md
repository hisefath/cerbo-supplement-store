## Ask

Payment reserves and releases stock

A payment for items no longer in stock is refused without charging, two patients can never both buy the last unit, a paid order lowers stock, and a failed payment gives its units back.

- Slice: vertical slice — EP-23 POST /api/orders/{orderId}/payments → BE-10 PaymentService.pay transaction (1) locks the product rows in id order and calls BE-13 StockService.reserveForOrder (a `sale` movement per line) → transaction (2b) on failure calls BE-13.releaseForOrder (`sale_release`) → `products.units_in_stock` and the new `stock_movements` table (SM-2, SEQ-2, INV-5)
- FR-026: "If a patient pays for an order whose items are no longer all in stock, then the system shall refuse the payment without charging and name the items short of stock."
- FR-027: "When an order is marked paid, the system shall reduce each product's stock by the quantity sold."
- AE-023: "Given 1 Vitamin D3 is in stock and Ana's order holds 2, When Ana pays, Then the payment is refused, nothing is charged and Vitamin D3 is named as short of stock."
- AE-024: "Given 1 Omega-3 is left and Ana and Ben each have an order for 1, When both pay at the same moment, Then exactly one payment succeeds and the other is refused without a charge."
- Out of this task: stock adjustments by platform staff and the stock page (M5-T004); the dashboard's stock figures (M5-T005).
- HITL: none
- Why claude: payment and stock, a money path with row locks; it runs on Claude next to M5-T003, whose Owns are disjoint.
